# P5.4.82B — Approval Quorum and Role Separation Decision Support

**Date:** 2026-11-04  
**Status:** OWNER-APPROVED — D82-B / ARCHITECTURE-POLICY ONLY  
**Scope:** D82-B policy design only. No identity enrollment, provider configuration, key operation, implementation or runtime authorization.

## 1. Governing owner decision

D82-A is approved: Independent escrow authority for recovery, combined with primary KMS/HSM for routine operations; low tolerance for loss of recovery capability. D82-A also constrains D82-B: at least two independent approvers are required, and requester and executor are excluded from approving the same recovery case.

This document proposes a concrete quorum and role model within those constraints. It does not treat “tiếp tục” as approval.

## 2. Options

| Option | Rule | Advantages | Risks |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — 2-of-2 fixed | Two named approvers must both approve | Simple, strict | One unavailable person blocks recovery; brittle succession |
| B — 2-of-3 role-based | Two of three pre-enrolled approvers, from distinct trust domains, approve | Balance of recoverability and dual control | Requires disciplined roster and succession |
| C — 3-of-5 | Three of five independent approvers approve | Higher resistance to one or two unavailable/compromised identities | Higher recovery latency and administrative burden |

## 3. Recommended default: Option B — 2-of-3 role-based

Pre-enroll three approval roles, each held by a distinct natural person and administered through a distinct trust domain:

1. **System-owner delegate:** owner-designated governance representative.
2. **Independent escrow custodian delegate:** representative of the independent escrow authority.
3. **Independent security/recovery delegate:** representative outside the requester, executor and primary KMS/HSM administration chain.

Two valid approvals are required. The two approvers must be distinct people and from distinct trust domains. Approval by the requester or assigned executor is invalid and does not count toward quorum. A person cannot occupy two approval slots for the same recovery case.

The three roles are functional slots, not names or selected organizations. Actual people, legal entities and provider accounts remain unselected.

## 4. Separation of duties

| Role | May do | Must not do |
|---|---|---|
| Requester | Open case, provide reason and requested scope | Approve own request; execute recovery solely by virtue of requesting |
| Approver | Approve/reject exact case digest within assigned role | Execute restore, access plaintext key material, approve altered scope |
| Executor | Perform the approved, narrowly scoped recovery operation | Approve the case or change its approved scope |
| Escrow authority | Enforce independent escrow policy and issue scoped authorization after quorum | Unilaterally bypass quorum or D6 gates |
| Primary KMS/HSM administrator | Routine service administration under separate controls | Act as sole recovery approver or obtain escrow authority |
| Auditor | Inspect immutable evidence and report violations | Approve or execute the operation being audited |

No one person may combine requester, approver and executor roles for one case. At minimum, requester and executor must be distinct from every counted approver.

## 5. Approval binding and replay protection

Each approval must bind to:
- Unique recovery case ID and one-time nonce.
- Canonical request digest and policy version.
- Exact Backup ID, immutable object identity/version and key-version references.
- Allowed operation and target scope.
- Expiry and approval time evidence.
- Approver identity, role slot, authentication context and independent trust domain.

Any change to scope, object, key version or operation invalidates existing approvals and requires a new case digest and quorum. Duplicate, expired, replayed, revoked or out-of-scope approvals are rejected. An approval is not a general authorization token and cannot be reused for another backup.

## 6. Unavailability, succession and emergency policy

**Recommended policy:** no single-person emergency bypass and no reduction below two independent approvers.

- Each of the three role slots may have a pre-designated alternate, enrolled and reviewed before an incident.
- Alternate activation must be authorized by the role's independent governance process, logged and effective before the alternate approves a case.
- No ad hoc enrollment or role transfer during an active recovery incident.
- If two eligible independent approvers cannot be assembled, the case remains `BLOCKED_QUORUM_UNAVAILABLE`.
- No verbal approval, chat message, GitHub review, or executor assertion substitutes for authenticated case-bound approval.
- An emergency can change incident priority and staffing, but cannot waive quorum, requester/executor separation, D3/D4/D5 verification or D6 gates.

## 7. Threat and availability trade-offs

Option B tolerates one unavailable approver while preserving two-person control, provided the requester/executor exclusions leave two eligible people in distinct trust domains. It does not guarantee recovery if two role holders are unavailable, if independence cannot be proven, or if all approval paths share a compromised identity provider. Those conditions remain blocked, not fail-open.

Option C is stronger against individual compromise but increases operational latency. Option A is not recommended because a single unavailable approver prevents recovery.

## 8. Owner decision record — D82-B (2026-11-04)

**APPROVED — WITHOUT AMENDMENTS, ARCHITECTURE/POLICY ONLY.** The owner explicitly selected:
1. **Option B — 2-of-3 role-based quorum** across three independent trust domains: system-owner delegate, independent escrow custodian delegate, and independent security/recovery delegate.
2. Two distinct people from distinct trust domains must approve the exact recovery case. Requester and executor are excluded from the counted quorum.
3. **Pre-designated alternates are allowed**, provided they are enrolled/designated and governed before an incident. No ad hoc alternate enrollment or role transfer during an active recovery case.
4. **No emergency bypass below quorum.** If two eligible independent approvers cannot be assembled, the case remains `BLOCKED_QUORUM_UNAVAILABLE`.
5. No verbal approval, GitHub review or executor assertion substitutes for authenticated, case-bound approval.

The three trust-domain roles are approved as the policy model only. Actual people, legal entities, custodian, jurisdiction, provider, account, alternate identities and governance implementation are not selected. No additional separate-legal-entity or separate-identity-provider requirement is inferred beyond the approved distinct trust-domain rule.
