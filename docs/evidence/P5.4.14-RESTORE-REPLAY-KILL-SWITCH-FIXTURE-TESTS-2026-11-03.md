## 1. Scope

Continued the previously authorized P5.4.8 fixture-only test plan. No production restore was attempted.

Three new tests were added to `ci/test_backup_restore.py`:
1. `test_duplicate_intent_retry_after_backup_does_not_duplicate_order_or_fill` — replaying an already-persisted intent after creating and reopening a fixture backup returns the exact original order and does not create another order, SUBMIT audit event, fill or consumed-book record.
2. `test_consumed_book_snapshot_cannot_be_reused_after_backup_restore` — after a successful fixture fill and backup, a distinct intent cannot reuse the already-consumed book snapshot after reopening the backup; it is rejected with `SNAPSHOT_ALREADY_USED`.
3. `test_restored_kill_latch_rejects_new_paper_entry` — a Kill Switch asserted before snapshot remains asserted after reopening the backup and blocks a new paper entry with `KILL_SWITCH`.

The tests use disposable SQLite files below each test's `TemporaryDirectory`. They instantiate the existing local PaperLedger against those fixture paths only; this is not a production restore drill.
