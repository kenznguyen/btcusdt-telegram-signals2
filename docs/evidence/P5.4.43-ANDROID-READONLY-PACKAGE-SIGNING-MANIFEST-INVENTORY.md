## 6. Implications for approved P5.4.41 decisions

The owner-approved topology remains:

- Unsuffixed regular app is the canonical host.
- Parallel/Paper app is an authenticated typed IPC client only.
- Common installed signing identity is required by policy.

Before implementation, the design must resolve a build/release signing plan that makes the actual installed regular host and parallel client share the approved signer/lineage.
