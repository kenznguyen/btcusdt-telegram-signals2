## 5. Minimum evidence needed to close material gaps

The following are evidence requirements, not requests to fabricate replacement records:

1. **Upstream/provider origin:** provider-issued export receipt or job metadata tied to the exact dataset/feed, symbol, time window and raw-object digest; independently obtained or verifiable through provider-controlled means.
2. **Collector execution:** original collector identity, software/version, execution/job ID, host or managed runtime identity, start/end timestamps and acquisition logs.
3. **Custody:** contemporaneous receipt/transfer/readback records from the earliest available acquisition boundary; actor, time, source and destination; no backfilled fictional events.
4. **Index authority:** named issuer, separately authenticated trust root, key authorization/purpose, validity/revocation/rotation evidence and canonical signed payload.
5. **Independent publication:** publication/receipt evidence in a control plane not writable solely by the producer or index issuer.
6. **Storage independence:** evidence that the custodian and index issuer do not share a single unreviewed administrative boundary; otherwise record the dependency and do not claim independence.
7. **Claim support:** mapping of each V2 claim to exact evidence objects, including contradictions, limitations and missing coverage.
8. **Completeness:** authoritative expected-set/schedule or an explicit bounded scope; endpoint pagination metadata alone is insufficient.

If original upstream or custody records never existed or cannot be recovered, retain UNKNOWN/UNAVAILABLE. Do not reconstruct them from the CSV's own fields.
