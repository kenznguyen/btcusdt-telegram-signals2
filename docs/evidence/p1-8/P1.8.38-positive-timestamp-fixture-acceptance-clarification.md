# P1.8.38 — Positive Timestamp Fixture Acceptance Clarification

**Version:** P1.8.38-v1  
**Type:** Documentation-only amendment to P1.8.36 fixture oracle  
**Depends on:** P1.8.26, P1.8.28, P1.8.34–P1.8.37  
**Status:** AMENDMENT RECORDED — V07 BLOCKED — PARAMETERS OPEN  
**Authorization:** Documentation only. No implementation, tests, key operation, provider selection, staging, data probe or workflow dispatch is authorized.

## 1. Purpose and precedence

This amendment resolves finding F-11 from P1.8.37. It narrows the expected outcomes of P1.8.36 fixtures TB-P01 and TB-P02 so that a successful cryptographic timestamp-binding check cannot be mistaken for approval of historical event acceptance.

This amendment is authoritative for the interpretation of TB-P01 and TB-P02. It does not rewrite the historical P1.8.36 record, change the approved policy direction in P1.8.34, or close V07.

## 2. Mandatory separation of outcomes

Every positive timestamp fixture MUST report separate results for these stages:

1. **BINDING_RESULT:** whether the timestamp token imprint matches the independently recomputed imprint over the exact signature bytes and required immutable context.
2. **TOKEN_VALIDATION_RESULT:** whether token signature, TSA chain, policy, validity and applicable trust checks pass under a frozen profile.
3. **EVENT_SIGNATURE_RESULT:** whether the event signature validates over the exact event bytes under the declared key and algorithm.
4. **HISTORICAL_ELIGIBILITY_RESULT:** whether the event is eligible under a separately frozen V07 key-validity and revocation policy.
5. **FINAL_DISPOSITION:** the policy outcome, including quarantine/hold where required.

A PASS in stages 1–3 MUST NOT be promoted automatically to historical eligibility or final acceptance. No fixture may collapse these stages into a single PASS.

Until the V07 profile, authoritative revocation source, effective-time precision, timestamp uncertainty treatment, equality semantics and required operational parameters are frozen and independently reviewed:
- BINDING_RESULT may be PASS when its own assertions pass.
- TOKEN_VALIDATION_RESULT and EVENT_SIGNATURE_RESULT may be reported only against a declared, frozen test profile; otherwise they are NOT VERIFIED/BLOCKED.
- HISTORICAL_ELIGIBILITY_RESULT MUST be BLOCKED / NOT DETERMINED.
- FINAL_DISPOSITION MUST be QUARANTINE / HOLD (or BLOCKED where no executable disposition exists); it MUST NOT be ACCEPT.
- No implementation may infer a missing policy value or treat an unknown revocation state as favorable.

## 3. Corrected positive fixture oracles

| ID | Scenario | Cryptographic fixture oracle | Historical eligibility and final disposition before V07 freeze |
|---|---|---|---|
| TB-P01 | Valid bound timestamp and event signature; trusted genTime is clearly earlier than the stated revocation time in the fixture data | Binding may PASS; token and event-signature checks are evaluated separately under a frozen test profile | BLOCKED / NOT DETERMINED; QUARANTINE / HOLD. A seemingly earlier genTime alone is not acceptance evidence while authority, uncertainty, revocation semantics and policy are OPEN. |
| TB-P02 | Same signed object has a valid bound timestamp before the stated revocation time but is first received after that time | Binding may PASS; token and event-signature checks are evaluated separately under a frozen test profile. Record genTime and received_at independently. | BLOCKED / NOT DETERMINED; QUARANTINE / HOLD. Late receipt does not rewrite trusted genTime, but does not bypass revocation-state freshness, authoritative policy, replay/idempotency or quarantine controls. |

After V07 is frozen, these fixtures may evaluate historical eligibility using only the approved policy version and frozen fixture bytes. Their expected final outcomes must then be explicitly versioned; this amendment does not preselect ACCEPT for either fixture.

## 4. Required evidence fields

For each fixture, keep separate fields for:
- exact event, signature and timestamp-token byte digests;
- recomputed imprint and immutable context digest;
- token validation profile/version and trust-policy reference;
- event-signature algorithm/profile and public-key fingerprint;
- TSA genTime and declared accuracy/uncertainty;
- first trusted received_at;
- authoritative revocation record/version and effective time/precision;
- revocation-state/cache freshness and retrieval provenance;
- V07 policy version;
- each stage result listed in §2;
- final disposition and deterministic reason code.

No private keys, credentials or secret material may be included. Client-supplied signed_at remains untrusted and MUST NOT substitute for TSA genTime.

## 5. Negative and boundary fixture continuity

P1.8.36 TB-N01–TB-N13 remain unchanged. In particular:
- TB-N07 remains ineligible for pre-revocation acceptance and fail-closed/quarantined.
- TB-N08 remains HOLD/quarantine while boundary precision, uncertainty and equality semantics are unresolved.
- TB-N09 remains reject/quarantine for invalid or unverifiable timestamp trust.
- TB-N12 remains idempotent and cannot create duplicate acceptance side effects.
- TB-N13 remains NOT PROVEN for historical pre-revocation existence and fail-closed/quarantined.

This amendment does not weaken any negative fixture or permit favorable inference from client clocks, receipt times, rounded timestamps or missing revocation evidence.

## 6. OPEN parameters preserved

All of the following remain OPEN; this amendment assigns no values:
- TSA type/provider, protocol/profile, policy OID and trust anchors;
- imprint encoding, canonicalization, digest algorithm and envelope schema;
- timestamp accuracy/uncertainty and trusted clock source;
- authoritative revocation source, effective-time precision and equality semantics;
- cache age, propagation bounds, rotation/cutover and quarantine roles;
- fixture bytes, fixture hashes, executable tests and reason-code registry;
- V08 watermark/lateness/boundary parameters;
- Restore failure-domain and RPO/RTO measurement details.
