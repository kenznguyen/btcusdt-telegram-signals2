**Version:** P1.8.47-v1

**Depends on:** P1.8.33–P1.8.46

**Authorization:** Documentation only. This register does not approve a provider, value, implementation, staging or operational action.

## 1. Purpose

This register separates:
1. Policy directions already explicitly approved.
2. Documentation decisions that can be confirmed without new measurements or provider access.
3. Decisions that cannot responsibly be proposed until evidence exists.
4. Implementation-specific decisions that depend on a separately approved provider/runtime choice.
5. Operational actions that require separate explicit permission.

The register is intended to prevent repeated requests for decisions that are already settled and to avoid asking the owner to guess values that should be evidence-derived.

## 2. Decision classes

- **APPROVED — DO NOT REOPEN:** binding policy direction already approved.
- **DOCUMENTATION READY:** wording/contract can be reviewed from existing evidence; no new operational permission needed.
- **EVIDENCE REQUIRED:** a measurement set or authoritative external contract is missing.
- **IMPLEMENTATION DEPENDENT:** cannot be finalized until an implementation/provider is separately selected and documented.
- **OWNER DECISION AFTER EVIDENCE:** owner must choose among explicit, evidence-backed alternatives.
- **SEPARATE PERMISSION REQUIRED:** operational privilege or side effect is involved; stop and ask before acting.

## 7. Explicit permission gates

The following are not covered by documentation approval and require a separate, explicit permission request naming exact scope:

1. Selecting or connecting to a TSA, revocation authority, key-management provider or database/storage provider.
2. Provisioning, importing, rotating, revoking or releasing keys; changing trust anchors or access controls.
3. Collecting data from live feeds, reading VPS/runtime state or running measurement probes against operational resources.
4. Provisioning or deploying isolated staging resources, networks, databases or credentials.
5. Running any crash/fault injection, restore rehearsal, destructive or non-destructive recovery test.
6. Dispatching any GitHub Actions workflow with operational or Git-write side effects.
7. Changing source/runtime, T0/T1, VPS, DB, Feeder, strategy, ledger, risk engine or live-execution controls.
8. Creating PRs or merging changes where not separately approved.

When such a gate is reached, stop before the action and request permission. State the exact action, target, scope, expected side effects, rollback, cost/resource bounds and evidence to be collected. Do not bundle multiple independent permissions into one vague request.
