## 1. Purpose and decision boundary

This record presents the owner decision required to resolve the authority-model gap identified by IR115-04. Approval to create this record is not itself a selection of a role model, named principal, delegation, compensating control, IAM permission or deletion authority.

Until the owner makes an explicit selection and the selected model is independently reviewed and proven in effective permissions, the governing default remains:
- no self-approval;
- no combining incompatible authority domains;
- no role assignment or access grant;
- no destructive workflow activation;
- no deletion authority.

## 2. Authority domains that must remain distinct

| Domain | Responsibility | Must not do |
|---|---|---|
| Restore authority | Authorize/reconcile restore epoch and recovery scope | Convert restore readiness into lifecycle approval |
| V07 trust/quarantine resolver | Resolve exact trust/revocation evidence | Grant deletion eligibility |
| TSA/V08 verifier | Verify evidence/checkpoint closure | Release dependencies or approve deletion |
| Consumer dependency owner | Release only its own exact dependency | Release another consumer's dependency/global hold |
| Lifecycle assessor | Assess eligibility under central policy | Approve or execute destructive mutation |
| Independent approver | Approve/reject immutable exact-scope package | Alter evidence, widen scope or execute storage mutation |
| Authorization issuer | Issue/revoke exact operation/object/generation/executor capability | Execute storage mutation or self-approve |
| Storage executor | Execute only the exact authorized mutation at the atomic fence | Decide eligibility, approve or clear holds |
| Independent verifier/auditor | Verify outcome and audit evidence | Rewrite operation history or grant retroactive authority |

A person, account, service or organization label is not proof of independence. Effective credentials, shared control, delegated privileges, break-glass access and indirect administrative paths must be included in the conflict analysis.

## 3. Decision options

### Option A — Independent multi-principal separation

Distinct principals and effective permission domains are assigned to assessment, approval, authorization, execution and verification. No principal can approve its own assessment or execute its own approval. Restore/trust verification remains separately controlled where conflicts could affect deletion eligibility.

**Advantages:** strongest separation; reduces unilateral misuse and review conflicts.

**Costs/constraints:** requires available independent principals, reliable identity lifecycle, delegation controls, coverage for absence and independently auditable permission boundaries.

**Evidence required before selection can become operational:** named accountable role owners; principal independence evidence; effective-permission graph; credential and delegation analysis; revocation/emergency-access proof; audit and verifier independence.

### Option B — Single-owner with explicit compensating controls

One owner may hold limited decision responsibilities only if a separately approved compensating-control design demonstrably prevents unreviewed unilateral destructive action. This is an exception, not the default.

At minimum, the proposed model must define:
- why independent approvers are unavailable and the exact scope of the exception;
- which functions remain technically impossible for the owner to combine;
- immutable pre-commit intent and evidence package;
- mandatory independent review/notification and a verifiable delay where appropriate;
- independent post-operation verification and retrospective review;
- out-of-band emergency stop and immediate revocation;
- conflict-of-interest, compromise and absence handling;
- suspension conditions and audit retention.

A self-attested checklist, second account controlled by the same person, delayed log review without an independent observer, or an unverified “break-glass” path is not sufficient evidence of compensation. This option MUST NOT be treated as approved merely because it is documented.

### Option C — No destructive authority until independent coverage exists

No principal is granted destructive approval/execution capability. The system may continue non-destructive analysis, read-only verification and documentation, but destructive lifecycle operations remain unavailable until Option A or a separately approved and verified Option B is feasible.

**Advantages:** preserves the strongest current safety boundary.

**Costs/constraints:** destructive lifecycle operations remain blocked; no operational deletion capability is available.

## 4. Decision matrix

| Question | Option A | Option B | Option C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independent approver exists | Required | May be unavailable, but compensation must be proven | Not required to preserve block |
| Self-approval | Prohibited | No unreviewed unilateral destructive action; exact exception controls must be proven | Prohibited |
| Separation of execution from approval | Required | Must remain technically enforced | No destructive executor enabled |
| IAM/role assignments | Only after separate implementation approval | Only after separate implementation approval and compensation review | None for destructive capability |
| Destructive capability | Only after complete qualification | Only after owner selection, independent review and proof | Disabled/not granted |

## 5. Required owner decision payload

The owner must explicitly select one:
- **A — Independent multi-principal separation**;
- **B — Single-owner compensating-control exception**, with a separate complete control proposal and justification;
- **C — No destructive authority until independent coverage exists**.

If A or B is selected, the owner must separately approve the exact named principals/role owners, scope, delegation rules, conflicts, emergency model and evidence required. No names, permissions, thresholds or controls are inferred by this record.

A response that merely approves this document or its preparation does not select A, B or C.

## 6. Default while decision is OPEN

Until an explicit owner selection and subsequent qualification:
- IR115-04 remains OPEN;
- no self-approval and no incompatible role combination;
- no IAM/access changes, credentials, workflow activation or destructive capability;
- lifecycle assessment, approval, authorization, execution and verification remain separate authority stages;
- restore READY, V07 resolution, TSA/V08 PASS and lifecycle assessment do not imply approval or execution authorization;
- no deletion authority is granted.
