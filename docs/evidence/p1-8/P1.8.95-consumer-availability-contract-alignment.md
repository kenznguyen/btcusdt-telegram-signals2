## 1. Purpose

This work resolves the gap between the logical availability declaration in P1.8.81 and the governing Dataset Consumer Contract. It does so by adding a normative, versioned documentary extension to the governing contract and recording the alignment decision here.

The change does not select an API, schema, registry technology, storage engine, archive provider, SLA, numeric threshold or runtime behavior. It does not assert that any current consumer has supplied an availability declaration.

## 3. Normative alignment decisions

1. **Exact scope:** availability is declared per consumer dependency and immutable dataset version, not for a mutable dataset alias.
2. **Accountability:** declaration is attributable to an authenticated consumer identity and accountable owner.
3. **Unknown remains protective:** missing, stale, contradictory, unverified or ownerless declaration is UNKNOWN and blocks archive review.
4. **Most protective compatible requirement:** the dataset's effective availability requirement is the most protective compatible requirement across active and unresolved dependencies. Incompatible declarations are CONFLICTED.
5. **ACK is unchanged:** ACK remains a processing statement, never release or archive/deletion authorization.
6. **Release is exact:** a consumer releases only its own dependency through the explicit release protocol.
7. **Revision is immutable:** changed declaration creates a new revision; it does not rewrite evidence already used in an earlier decision.
8. **Decision invalidation:** a material declaration change after archive approval or during final recheck invalidates that archive decision.
9. **Archive is separately governed:** archive requires exact-version restore/recovery evidence and separate eligibility/authorization. Archive authorization is not deletion authorization.
10. **No guessed values:** numeric access latency, restore delay, freshness, review interval, trusted time, provider and registry properties remain OPEN.

## 4. Required declaration contract

The governing contract §20 now requires, as applicable:
- dependency and immutable dataset/version IDs;
- consumer identity, owner and purpose;
- access pattern and qualitative availability class;
- owner-supplied maximum access latency and restore delay where applicable;
- access window, completeness and consistency requirements;
- restore semantics, degraded-mode behavior and recovery evidence;
- immutable declaration revision, owner attestation and trusted event references.

These are logical contract fields, not a physical schema. A field cannot be silently omitted; NOT_APPLICABLE requires a reason and owner attribution.

## 5. Archive admission gate

Archive review remains BLOCKED unless every active and unresolved dependency has a complete owner-attributed declaration, no suspected unregistered consumer remains, the proposed path meets all combined requirements, exact-version restore/integrity evidence exists where required, and separate archive authorization is present.

This contract alignment does not relax any existing fail-closed rule. It does not resolve registry technology, registry recovery, unregistered-consumer discovery, runtime inventory or provider-specific archive semantics.

## 7. Remaining OPEN items

- Numeric access-latency and restore-delay bounds per consumer.
- Declaration freshness/review interval and trusted time source.
- Registry technology, consistency, recovery and durable revision proof.
- Discovery authority and completeness for unregistered/bypass consumers.
- Provider-specific archive/restore semantics and independent proof.
- Runtime declarations for actual T0–T4 consumers.
- Identity, owner attestation and delegation mechanism.

No default values or operational authority are introduced.
