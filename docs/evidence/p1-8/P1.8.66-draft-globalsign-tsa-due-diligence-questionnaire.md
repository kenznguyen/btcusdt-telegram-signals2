## Draft inquiry

**Subject:** Technical due-diligence questions — RFC 3161 non-qualified timestamp service for event-integrity evidence

Hello GlobalSign team,

We are evaluating RFC 3161 timestamping for integrity evidence associated with a private, non-commercial event ledger. The service would receive only a cryptographic message imprint; no event payload, trading data, personal data, private key or signing credential would be submitted.

Before considering any technical evaluation, we would appreciate written answers to the questions below and links to the currently applicable policy and practice documents.

### A. Service scope and contractual use
1. Does the current non-qualified RFC 3161 timestamp service and policy OID `1.3.6.1.4.1.4146.2.3` permit timestamping arbitrary opaque event/ledger hashes, rather than only software, PDF or code-signing artifacts?
2. Which current endpoint is designated for that use, and what transport/security requirements apply?
3. Which current Timestamping Practice Statement, Timestamp Policy, CPS and service terms govern the endpoint? Please provide version numbers, effective dates and change-notification method.
4. Are there restrictions on automated/high-frequency requests, commercial/non-commercial use, geographic access or long-term retention?

### B. Accuracy and token profile
5. Does the published one-second accuracy statement remain current for the non-qualified service? What is the precise meaning of the bound (maximum deviation, confidence/coverage, measurement method and exclusions)?
6. Is the accuracy value included in every RFC 3161 token's `TSTInfo.accuracy` field? If not, which authoritative policy document supplies it and how can a relying party preserve that policy evidence?
7. What imprint hash algorithms and timestamp-token signature algorithms/key sizes are currently accepted/issued? Are any algorithms deprecated or scheduled for retirement?
8. Which policy OID(s) are emitted for each service class? Can the requested policy be pinned in the RFC 3161 request?
9. Is `ordering` supported or set in issued tokens? What ordering guarantees, if any, apply across separate timestamping units?

### C. Nonce, replay and response behavior
10. Does the current service echo an RFC 3161 request nonce exactly whenever present, as required by RFC 3161?
11. How are malformed requests, unsupported hash algorithms, unsupported policy OIDs and duplicate/replayed requests handled?
12. Are there documented response, retry, rate-limit and error semantics suitable for a deterministic client?

### D. Certificate lifecycle and historical verification
13. Please provide the current TSA signer certificate(s), issuing chain, trust-anchor references, certificate policy/EKU profile and publication location.
14. What is the current certificate rollover schedule? Is there an overlap period, advance notice and a stable historical repository for retired signer certificates and chains?
15. How can relying parties obtain historical revocation evidence (signed OCSP responses and/or CRLs) for a retired or expired TSA signer certificate?
16. Is an OCSP responder available for each signer chain? Please identify the authoritative AIA/OCSP and CRL Distribution Point locations and explain delegated responder authorization and responder-certificate revocation handling.
17. How long are timestamp tokens, signer certificates, historical status evidence and relevant audit records retained or made available?
18. Is there a timestamp-token log or other mechanism to verify token authenticity and issuance after certificate expiry or service migration?

### E. Availability, operations and commercial terms
19. What service availability commitment, maintenance notice, incident notification and support response terms apply to this service?
20. What request-volume limits, pricing, minimum commitments or other fees apply?
21. Which legal entity provides the service, which jurisdiction governs the service terms, and what data-processing terms apply to a request containing only a one-way imprint?
22. Can GlobalSign provide a written statement confirming the intended use and the current accuracy/profile commitments for the exact non-qualified endpoint?

We are not requesting credentials, private keys, production access or a live test at this stage. Please do not provision an account or enable a service on our behalf. A written response and current public documents are sufficient for this diligence phase.

Thank you.
