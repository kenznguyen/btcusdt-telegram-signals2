## 5. Findings

### IR115-01 — Restore governance revision vector is not explicitly complete against P1.8.93
**Severity: MAJOR — PARTIAL**

P1.8.110 §2 defines the Governance revision vector as policy, dependency, hold, revocation, tombstone and authorization-revocation revisions. P1.8.110 §3 binds that vector to the restore reconciliation cut.

P1.8.93 §2 protects additional decision inputs, including each applicable consumer availability-declaration revision/owner-attestation reference and the copy/backup/recovery disposition revision. P1.8.110 does not explicitly require those revision domains, or an equivalent complete P1.8.93 vector digest, to be included in the restore-boundary binding.

The exact object/copy manifest digest in P1.8.110 is not a substitute for the authoritative revision of the copy/recovery disposition decision. Likewise, a dependency-registry revision does not necessarily prove that each consumer availability declaration and owner attestation was reconciled at the same cut.

**Required closure:** define the restore-boundary governance vector as a versioned, complete crosswalk to every applicable P1.8.93 protected-vector revision/reference, including consumer availability declarations and copy/backup/recovery disposition. If a field is intentionally represented by another authoritative revision, specify that binding and prove it. Missing or non-coincident revisions remain QUARANTINED.

### IR115-02 — Trusted-time freshness is not explicitly recomputed at mutation linearization
**Severity: MODERATE — PARTIAL**

P1.8.112 §2 binds observation time, freshness-policy revision and a measured age result. §3 invalidates the vector when freshness policy, observed age or uncertainty disposition changes and requires final re-evaluation of pinned revisions at the P1.8.93 mutation linearization point.

A pinned observation and its preflight age result do not remain equally fresh as time advances. The contract does not explicitly state that the final fence obtains or validates a trusted current-time observation and recomputes age/uncertainty against the applicable freshness policy at the actual mutation boundary. Rechecking only that the old evidence revision is unchanged could accept evidence that became stale after preflight.

**Required closure:** state that freshness is evaluated using an authoritative trusted-time observation bound to the final mutation decision, with age and uncertainty recomputed at that boundary. If a fresh observation or atomic/transactionally coupled recheck cannot be established, the decision is HOLD and the adapter remains INELIGIBLE. No freshness value or provider is selected by this finding.

### IR115-03 — TSA graph edge direction and canonical closure semantics remain underspecified
**Severity: MODERATE — PARTIAL**

P1.8.113 §4 defines typed edges and requires source/target node versions, but it does not normatively define the orientation for each edge type or a canonical traversal rule. For example, the graph representation could encode CLAIM_REQUIRES as claim-to-evidence or evidence-to-claim, and COPY_OF as copy-to-source or source-to-copy. A reader cannot derive a unique incoming-edge test or deterministic transitive closure from the current text alone.

P1.8.113 §7 and §8 correctly require complete graph evidence and make unknown edges protective. The copy lifecycle contract also requires a source hold to protect every known copy containing, representing or deriving from the held scope unless an authorized reviewer explicitly proves that the hold does not apply to the exact copy.

**Required closure:** define edge orientation and traversal semantics per edge type; specify the graph root/scope and the exact completeness proof required before an absence-of-edge claim; explicitly bind source-hold applicability to all known descendant/derived copies, with exclusion allowed only by the authorized, evidence-bound exception in the governing copy contract. Until then, graph disposition remains HOLD when traversal or inheritance is ambiguous.

### IR115-04 — P1.8.114 does not resolve the owner role-model decision required by IR109-05
**Severity: MAJOR — OPEN BY DESIGN**

P1.8.114 provides a useful non-escalation matrix and correctly states that restore READY, V07 resolution, TSA verification and V08 closure do not imply lifecycle eligibility, approval or execution authorization.

However, P1.8.114 §§5 and 8 explicitly leave undecided:
- independent approver availability and independence evidence;
- whether a single-owner compensating-control model is acceptable;
- named principal/role ownership and delegation;
- lifecycle assessor, approval authority, execution-authorization issuer, storage executor and independent verifier assignments;
- emergency access and conflict handling.

This is safe as a boundary record, but it is not the owner role-model decision requested by IR109-05. The prior finding must therefore remain PARTIAL/OPEN rather than being marked closed. The approval to author P1.8.114 did not select a single-owner exception or provision any permission.

**Required closure:** a separate explicit owner decision on the role model, supported by a conflict-of-interest and effective-permission analysis. Until then, retain the default no-self-approval/no-combination rule and do not assign roles or activate IAM/workflows.
