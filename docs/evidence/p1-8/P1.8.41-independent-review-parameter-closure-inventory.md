## 3. Findings

### F-12 — MEDIUM: V07 trusted-time profile sub-items are not individually enumerated

P1.8.33 and P1.8.36 identify separate unresolved elements that P1.8.40 currently groups into V07-D1/D2. The inventory should explicitly track, at minimum:

1. Trust-anchor distribution, lifecycle, rollover and independent validation.
2. Timestamp protocol/profile, policy OID and accepted token format.
3. Token certificate/path validation and applicable historical trust/revocation checks.
4. Imprint digest algorithm, domain separation, canonical encoding and exact envelope/context field set.
5. Timestamp accuracy/uncertainty, trusted clock source and boundary treatment.
6. Token nonce requirements and timestamp-token replay/duplicate semantics.
7. Fixture byte material, token/signature bytes, recomputed imprint and immutable expected digest/oracle.

These details are not approved or measured values. Their absence as separate traceable closure rows makes it harder to prove that every P1.8.36 OPEN item has an owner, evidence requirement and exit condition.

**Required disposition:** Add explicit sub-items under V07-D1/D2 (or a linked sub-register), preserve all as OPEN, and map each to evidence and review dependencies. Do not choose a provider, algorithm or numeric threshold.

### F-13 — LOW: cross-document evidence provenance should be explicit for every V07 sub-item

P1.8.40 records the P1.8.33 decision IDs but does not provide a compact source-clause mapping for the additional P1.8.36 cryptographic profile requirements. The amendment should map each V07 sub-item to its originating P1.8.33/P1.8.34/P1.8.36/P1.8.38 section or fixture IDs. This is a documentation traceability improvement, not a new policy decision.
