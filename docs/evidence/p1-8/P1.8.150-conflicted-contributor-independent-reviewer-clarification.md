# P1.8.150 — Conflicted Contributor versus Qualifying Independent Reviewer Clarification

## 1. Purpose and precedence

This supplement resolves IR149-01 by distinguishing a conflicted contributor from a qualifying independent reviewer and defining who may issue the review disposition.

Apply this supplement with P1.8.148, P1.8.146 §6 and P1.8.144. It clarifies terminology and authority only. It does not weaken any stricter separation requirement, amend P1.8.143 RET-01 B / RET-02 B, or weaken Option C — No Destructive Authority. In a conflict, the stricter protective rule applies.

This is a documentary rule for future assessments. It does not authorize identity/personnel discovery, permission/API inventory, operational assessment, implementation, tests, workflow dispatch or data mutation.

## 2. Two distinct capacities

For each claim, the assessment MUST distinguish:

**A. Contributor / evidence producer / conflicted participant**
- May supply source records, factual attestations within their authority, provenance explanations, technical context or a preliminary analysis.
- If conflicted under P1.8.148 §2, MUST be labelled **CONFLICTED CONTRIBUTOR** for that claim.
- May not be described as an independent reviewer, primary independent reviewer, verifier or accepter.
- May not issue, approve, co-sign or determine the independent review disposition.
- Any preliminary analysis must be labelled **NON-INDEPENDENT — NON-AUTHORITATIVE — NOT AN ACCEPTANCE**.
- Their contribution is evidence/input only and remains subject to independent examination.

**B. Qualifying independent reviewer**
- MUST have none of the conflicting roles identified by P1.8.148 §2 for the assessed claim.
- MUST satisfy competence, evidence-control independence and direct-examination requirements in P1.8.148 §§3–5.
- Is the sole person authorized by this reviewer-control contract to issue the independent review disposition for that claim.
- MUST independently examine the underlying evidence and may accept only the narrow documentary claim supported by that evidence, or retain/reinstate BLOCKED / UNKNOWN.
- MUST not rely solely on a conflicted contributor's summary, attestation or preliminary conclusion.

A person cannot occupy both capacities for the same claim. A conflict is claim-specific but must be assessed against actual decision rights, control, incentives and participation, not job title or nominal organizational separation.

## 3. Meaning of “second reviewer” when a conflict exists

Where P1.8.148's full role separation is infeasible because a proposed primary participant is conflicted, the phrase **independent second reviewer** is procedural sequencing terminology only. It does not imply that the conflicted participant is a qualifying first reviewer.

For the affected claim:
1. The conflicted participant, if involved, is only a CONFLICTED CONTRIBUTOR and may provide a clearly labelled non-authoritative preliminary analysis.
2. The independent second reviewer becomes the **sole qualifying independent reviewer** for that claim.
3. That reviewer must independently inspect the underlying evidence, exact scope/version, provenance, completeness, role declarations, limitations and applicable contract requirements.
4. Only that qualifying reviewer may record the independent review disposition.
5. The conflicted contributor cannot accept, veto, co-sign or override that disposition.

If no qualifying independent reviewer is available, the claim MUST remain **INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_REQUIRED / BLOCKED**. A second conflicted person, mutual approval, seniority, elapsed time or automated check cannot substitute for a qualifying reviewer.

## 4. Required record and evidence binding

For each claim, record:
- claim identifier and exact object/version/copy/graph scope;
- each participant's capacity: qualifying independent reviewer, conflicted contributor, evidence producer, custodian, obligation owner or other relevant role;
- role/conflict matrix, decision rights, evidence control and conflict determination;
- any conflicted preliminary analysis, explicitly labelled non-independent and non-authoritative;
- qualifying reviewer's competence basis and independence declaration;
- exact evidence identifiers, immutable/version references and provenance;
- proof that the qualifying reviewer directly examined underlying evidence;
- independent findings, limitations, dissent, rationale and final disposition;
- review timestamp and applicable policy/contract versions.

A conflicted contribution must never be represented in the record as an independent review. If role status or evidence control is unknown, the reviewer qualification is NOT ESTABLISHED and the claim remains BLOCKED.

## 5. Fail-closed and no acceptance by proxy

The claim MUST remain **INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_REQUIRED / BLOCKED** where:
- a conflicted participant is presented as a reviewer or disposition authority;
- the qualifying reviewer's independence, competence or evidence-control separation is not established;
- the qualifying reviewer did not directly examine underlying evidence;
- the disposition depends solely on a conflicted person's analysis or attestation;
- evidence scope, version, provenance or completeness cannot be bound;
- unresolved disagreement or material limitation prevents a supported conclusion.

No approval, signature, recommendation or preliminary conclusion from a conflicted contributor can be counted as independent acceptance. The qualifying reviewer may cite a conflicted contribution as evidence input, but must independently assess it and state its limitations.
