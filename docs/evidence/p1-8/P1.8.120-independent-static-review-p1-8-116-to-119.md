## 5. Finding

### IR120-01 — Option B lacks a mandatory pre-commit independent veto/control
**Severity: MAJOR — OPTION B NOT SELECTION-READY**

P1.8.119 §3 Option B allows a single-owner compensating-control exception only if it demonstrably prevents unreviewed unilateral destructive action. Its minimum controls then describe “mandatory independent review/notification and a verifiable delay where appropriate”, independent post-operation verification and retrospective review.

The qualification “where appropriate” leaves open whether independent review and delay are mandatory before the destructive commit. Notification or retrospective review after commit cannot prevent a single owner from executing an unsafe action. A second account controlled by the same person is already correctly rejected, but the document does not yet specify the minimum independent pre-commit veto/control, its authority to block, its exact timing relative to the P1.8.93 mutation fence, or the fail-closed result when that control is unavailable.

**Required closure before Option B can be selected:** define an independently controlled, technically enforced pre-commit control that can block the exact operation before the storage mutation linearization point; define the evidence and ordering binding to the immutable approval package and P1.8.93 fence; define unavailable/conflicting/revoked reviewer behavior as HOLD; and retain independent post-operation verification. If such a control cannot be provided, Option B is unavailable and the owner must choose A or C. Do not treat this finding as permission to assign roles or activate IAM.

**Current authority effect:** none. P1.8.119 explicitly selects no option, assigns no principal and grants no access. The default no-self-approval/no-incompatible-role-combination/no-destructive-authority boundary remains in force.
