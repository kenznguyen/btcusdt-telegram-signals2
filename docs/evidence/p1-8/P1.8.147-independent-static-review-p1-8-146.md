## 3. Finding

### IR147-01 — MODERATE: Reviewer independence from custodian, obligation owner and approver is not mandatory enough

P1.8.146 §6 requires recording whether the reviewer had requester, custodian, obligation-owner, approver, executor or provider-selection roles. It also requires independence rationale and a compensating control if full role separation is infeasible.

However, the operative independence sentence mandates independence from the requester and any future executor, while the evidence-creator prohibition only prevents the creator/controller of underlying evidence from being the sole verifier. It does not clearly require the reviewer to be independent of the custodian, obligation owner and approver, nor define a mandatory independent second-review control when those roles overlap.

This leaves a gap against IR145-05's requested separation from requester/custodian/approver/executor. Merely recording a conflict does not resolve it.

Required narrow closure:
- require the reviewer to be independent of requester, custodian, obligation owner, approver and future executor for the claims being accepted;
- if organizational separation is infeasible, require a separately identified independent reviewer/control that has no such conflicting role and explicitly reviews the affected evidence and decision;
- prohibit a conflicted person from being the sole reviewer or verifier;
- preserve specialist/legal attribution and avoid requiring disclosure of unnecessary personal data.

This is a documentary clarification only. It does not require naming or inspecting any real person, account, identity or permission.
