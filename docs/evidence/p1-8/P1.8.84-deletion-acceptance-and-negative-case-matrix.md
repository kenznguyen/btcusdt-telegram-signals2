## 1. Purpose and boundary

This matrix consolidates the design and safety gates from P1.8.79–P1.8.83 and the governing lifecycle contracts. It defines what must be true before a deletion/copy-disposition design could be accepted, and what the system must do when evidence or controls fail.

It is an acceptance specification, not an implementation, test report, runtime inventory, certification, or authorization. A row marked DESIGN_DEFINED means only that a requirement is documented. It does not mean the corresponding runtime control has been implemented or tested.

## 3. End-to-end acceptance gates

A future implementation must pass every applicable gate in order. Failure at any gate blocks all later gates.

| Gate | Required acceptance condition | Minimum evidence | Failure result |
|---|---|---|---|
| G0 — Scope and authority | Purpose, requester, source owner, operation and environment boundary are explicit | Request record, identity and scope statement | BLOCKED |
| G1 — Classification and retention | Exact dataset/version class and approved retention/disposition policy are known | Classification, policy version, owner decision | BLOCKED / OPEN |
| G2 — Exact source identity | Immutable dataset/version/object identity and canonical manifest are established | Manifest bytes, digest, object generations and canonicalization version | BLOCKED |
| G3 — Dependency safety | Every consumer dependency is authoritative, current and reconciled; no unknown/unregistered consumer | Registry decision revision, consumer-specific evidence and release attestations | BLOCKED |
| G4 — Holds and obligations | Legal, incident, security, audit, research, recovery and operational holds are resolved | Hold register and authorized release/exception evidence | BLOCKED |
| G5 — Copy inventory | All applicable replicas, backups, exports, local copies, caches, derived outputs and external destinations are accounted for | Per-boundary completeness evidence and exact copy lineage | BLOCKED / NOT VERIFIED |
| G6 — Archive/rebuild/recovery | Archive compatibility, rebuildability and retained recovery points are independently demonstrated | Consumer availability requirements, reproducibility evidence, backup chain and restore proof | BLOCKED |
| G7 — Eligibility review | Eligibility reviewer evaluates all current evidence without changing it | Signed/attributable review and evidence snapshot | BLOCKED |
| G8 — Independent approval | Approver is independent of requester; required role separation and decision are valid | Identity/delegation evidence, exact package approval | BLOCKED |
| G9 — Final execution authorization | Current conditions rechecked; authorization binds exact manifest, operation, adapter and executor | Narrow authorization, final registry/hold snapshot, idempotency key | BLOCKED |
| G10 — Adapter preflight | Adapter enforces immutable identity, exact scope, preconditions, concurrency and fail-closed behavior | Adapter capability evidence and negative-test results | BLOCKED |

## 5. Cross-domain invariants

The following invariants must hold across every stage:

### 5.1 Dataset and consumer domain
- Dataset lifecycle, each consumer dependency, copy lifecycle, deletion request/execution and evidence lifecycle remain separate state domains.
- Consumer ACK is not dependency release; dependency release is not deletion approval.
- Stale/unreachable/unknown consumer state remains protective.
- Registry outage, inconsistent commit, uncertain authority or unregistered consumer suspicion blocks disposition.
- Archive cannot proceed when consumer availability, latency, completeness, consistency or restore requirements are unknown.

### 5.2 Copy and recovery domain
- Source, each copy, and each recovery point have independent identities and decisions.
- No cascade to backups, replicas, exports, caches, model artifacts, local copies or evidence.
- Source hold inheritance is protective; exclusions require exact-scope authorized evidence.
- Recovery-point integrity includes its entire base/incremental/log/metadata/key chain.
- Restore success does not authorize deletion; upload/checksum success does not prove restore.

### 5.3 Authorization and execution domain
- Approval, execution authorization, execution and independent verification are separate gates.
- Exact manifest digest, policy version, operation, adapter and executor are bound to authorization.
- Any material change invalidates approval.
- Executor has no policy, dependency-release, scope-expansion or self-approval authority.
- No blind retry, inferred rollback or ambiguous success.
- Independent verifier must establish actual outcome and collateral-effect absence.

### 5.4 Evidence domain
- Decision, execution, verification, lineage, recovery and operational evidence are distinct classes.
- Tombstone is metadata, not payload and not standalone proof of deletion.
- Evidence store failure blocks completion.
- Audit/evidence disposition is separately governed and cannot cascade from payload deletion.
- No sensitive payload, credentials, tokens, private keys or unnecessary personal data in tombstones.

## 6. Layer-specific protected boundaries

| Layer/domain | Protected invariant | What must not be inferred |
|---|---|---|
| T0 canonical market data | Independent data asset; source identity, provenance, writer fencing and recovery obligations remain protected | No general deletion right from retention review, maintenance, consumer ACK or copy disposition |
| T1 raw/observed data | Source observations, snapshots and sidecar evidence require class/lineage/consumer review | “Raw”, “old” or “temporary” does not mean disposable |
| T2 data/model pipeline | Inputs, features, labels, training runs, models, predictions and lineage remain traceable | Rebuildability or model replacement is not automatic deletion permission |
| T3 paper trading | CORE_LEDGER and AI_LEDGER remain separate; orders, reservations, risk, account/equity, funding, audit and event history protected by obligations | Ledger cleanup cannot be inferred from strategy inactivity or derived reports |
| T4 read models/UI | Read-only presentation boundary; derived cache status does not imply no independent consumers | UI or cache TTL does not authorize underlying data deletion |
| CI/research evidence | Commit, workflow, test and artifact provenance remain distinct from runtime evidence | CI artifact does not prove production state, backup or restore |
| Backups/replicas/exports | Independent copy identities and recovery/dependency obligations | Source decision does not cascade to copies |
| Governance/audit/tombstones | Independent evidence lifecycle | Payload deletion does not authorize evidence deletion |
