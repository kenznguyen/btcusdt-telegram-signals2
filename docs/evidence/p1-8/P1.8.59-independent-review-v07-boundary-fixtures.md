## 2. Finding F-16 — OCSP nonce omission is profile-dependent

**Severity:** MEDIUM  
**Affected cases:** V07-NONCE-03, V07-NONCE-04

RFC 9654 requires requesters implementing the updated extension to use a minimum 32-octet nonce. A supporting responder must accept nonce lengths from 16 through 32 octets, but may choose to omit the response nonce for request values from 1–15 or 33–128 octets. Thus, omission cannot be globally treated as either success or failure without specifying the negotiated requester/responder profile and project replay policy.

**Required clarification:**
- Split response nonce behavior into exact echo, permitted omission under the responder's declared profile, and prohibited/required-echo omission under a stricter project profile.
- A permitted RFC 9654 omission must not be mislabeled as cryptographic nonce-echo PASS; replay/freshness must then be established by the remaining approved mechanism or remain NOT VERIFIED.
- Keep project profile, downgrade policy and acceptance behavior OPEN.

Primary source: RFC 9654, https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9654.html
