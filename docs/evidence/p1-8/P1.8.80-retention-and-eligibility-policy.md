# P1.8.80 — Retention and Eligibility Policy

Date: 2026-11-09

## 1. Purpose and authority

This document decomposes the Central Data Lifecycle Policy into a class-by-class retention and disposition decision framework for T0–T4 and related evidence/copy domains.

It is a design supplement to:
- `docs/architecture/CENTRAL_DATA_LIFECYCLE_POLICY.md`;
- `docs/architecture/DATA_DELETION_SAFETY_CONTRACT.md`;
- `docs/architecture/DATASET_CONSUMER_CONTRACT.md`;
- `docs/architecture/DATA_COPY_BACKUP_REPLICA_LIFECYCLE_CONTRACT.md`;
- `docs/architecture/DATASET_CONSUMER_INVENTORY_BASELINE_2026-11-09.md`.

This document does not activate a retention timer, archive schedule, cleanup job, compaction, vacuum, deletion executor, or storage lifecycle rule. It does not designate an official environment or verify runtime inventory.

**Authority rule:** a class marked REVIEWABLE or CONDITIONAL is only eligible to enter a read-only review. No class in this document is authorized for deletion. Eligibility is not approval; approval is not execution authorization.

## 2. Normative principles

1. Retention is demand- and obligation-based, not age-based.
2. The effective preservation end is the latest unresolved end condition across operational, consumer, reproducibility, audit, incident, recovery, legal, and hold obligations.
3. If an obligation has no approved end condition, preserve the data and leave the policy decision OPEN; do not invent a duration.
4. Unknown classification, steward, provenance, dependency, hold, lineage, copy inventory, or recovery state blocks destructive disposition.
5. Storage pressure, low access frequency, no recent reads, consumer ACK, job completion, lease expiry, heartbeat loss, or elapsed time never independently authorize archive or deletion.
6. Archive is a storage/availability transition, not deletion. Unknown or incompatible consumer availability requirements block archive.
7. Rebuildability must be demonstrated with exact source versions, transformation/model versions, inputs, and integrity comparison. A claim that data can be regenerated is insufficient.
8. Copies have separate lifecycle identities. No implicit cascade from source to backup, replica, snapshot, export, cache, evidence, derived output, model, or ledger.
9. T0 canonical data remains protected by default. This policy does not decide absolute non-deletability versus a narrowly governed exception.
10. CORE_LEDGER and AI_LEDGER remain independently attributable; lifecycle convenience must not merge or net their histories.
11. Policy changes are versioned and prospective. A less protective rule cannot silently weaken an existing hold or obligation.
12. The more protective applicable rule governs while policy conflicts remain unresolved.

## 3. Disposition vocabulary

| Decision | Meaning | Permitted effect |
|---|---|---|
| PROTECTED | A known operational, canonical, audit, recovery, or other obligation requires preservation | No destructive lifecycle transition |
| RETAIN_UNTIL_REVIEW | Preserve until a named owner resolves a specific obligation | Review/escalation only; no automatic release |
| ARCHIVE_REVIEWABLE | Archive may be evaluated if availability, restore, integrity, security, and authorization gates pass | Read-only archive assessment; no archive execution permission |
| DELETION_REVIEWABLE | A narrowly scoped request may enter deletion eligibility review after all class-specific prerequisites are evidenced | Read-only eligibility review only; no deletion permission |
| BLOCKED_UNKNOWN | Evidence, inventory, ownership, or policy is missing, stale, contradictory, or unavailable | Fail closed; no archive or deletion |
| OPEN_OWNER_DECISION | A policy decision is intentionally reserved to the project owner | No default may be inferred |

These are policy outcomes, not runtime enums. A dataset can have several simultaneous obligations; the most protective outcome controls.

### 4.1 Interpretation limits

- “Conditional” and “reviewable” do not mean that an object is presently eligible.
- No class is assigned a numeric retention duration, minimum preservation floor, grace period, or expiry timestamp by this document.
- No dataset class is granted automatic cleanup or deletion.
- The static inventory does not establish actual files, databases, rows, backups, replicas, exports, consumers, or active processes.
- Any dataset with conflicting classification or overlapping obligations inherits the more protective outcome until resolved.

## 5. Retention calculation and review model

For a dataset version, the policy review must enumerate applicable obligations independently:

- operational use and canonical authority;
- registered consumer dependencies and declared purpose;
- replay, backtest, training, evaluation, inference and model reproducibility;
- source validation, correction, reprocessing and data-quality disputes;
- signal, risk, ledger, execution and reconciliation evidence;
- incident, security, legal, contractual and audit holds;
- recovery, backup-chain and restore obligations;
- copy-specific dependencies and source-hold inheritance.

The effective preservation end is not computed from a guessed age threshold. It remains unresolved while any applicable obligation is active, unknown, disputed, or lacks an approved end condition.

A review deadline may create an owner task or escalation. It cannot release a dependency, expire a hold, mark a dataset disposable, or trigger a storage operation.

## 6. Archive eligibility gates

Archive review is allowed only when all of the following are evidenced for the exact dataset version:

1. Identity, owner/steward, class, sensitivity, provenance, lineage and integrity are established.
2. Every consumer's online, latency, restore-time and access requirements are declared in an authoritative contract.
3. The proposed archive and restore service meets those requirements; unknown requirements block.
4. No active dependency, hold, incident, correction, audit, legal, recovery or reproducibility obligation prohibits the transition.
5. Archive destination, access controls and encryption are approved for the sensitivity class.
6. Restore procedure and independent integrity verification have non-production evidence.
7. Manifests, lineage, policy identity and audit evidence remain discoverable.
8. Exact-scope archive authorization and interrupted-operation reconciliation are defined.

Archive eligibility is not archive authorization. Archive completion does not make the source eligible for deletion.

The normative consumer availability vocabulary remains a gap to be closed in P1.8.81. Until then, archive is blocked wherever a consumer requirement is unknown.

## 7. Deletion eligibility gates

A class may enter a future deletion eligibility review only if all applicable conditions are satisfied:

1. A policy version explicitly permits review for that class and exact scope.
2. Dataset/version/object identity and immutable manifest are complete.
3. Steward, classification, provenance and source lineage are authoritative.
4. Every consumer dependency is released by its owner or resolved through separately approved adjudication.
5. No operational, replay, training, inference, backtest, audit, incident, legal, security, correction, recovery or hold obligation remains.
6. Rebuildability is demonstrated where relied upon, using exact retained inputs and transformations.
7. All copies and backup-chain impacts are identified and governed independently.
8. Archive or access restriction has been evaluated as a less destructive alternative.
9. Required tombstone and audit evidence can be preserved independently.
10. Evidence freshness, registry consistency, approver model and adapter safeguards have approved contracts.
11. A separate deletion safety review, independent approval, final execution authorization and post-operation verifier are available.

Failure or uncertainty at any gate means BLOCKED, not “best effort”. Even after all gates pass, only a read-only eligibility result may be produced; approval and execution require the separate contracts and explicit owner authorization.

## 8. Holds, conflicts and exceptional requests

- Legal, privacy, security, incident, audit, recovery and owner-directed holds are independently evaluated.
- A hold applies to the exact source scope and known copies/derivatives according to lineage; unknown lineage remains protected.
- Hold expiry or review date does not auto-release the hold.
- Conflicting policy versions resolve to the more protective applicable rule pending review.
- Emergency or legal requests do not bypass identity, exact scope, evidence, audit, independent review or authorization.
- The competent authority and legal basis for exceptional requests remain OPEN; this document does not interpret legal duties.
