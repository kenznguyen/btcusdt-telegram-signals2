# P1.8.117 — Trusted-Time Freshness at Mutation Linearization

**Version:** P1.8.117-v1  
**Closes:** IR115-02 (documentary design clarification)  
**Status:** DOCUMENTARY CONTRACT — NOT IMPLEMENTED / NOT ACTIVE / NOT AUTHORIZED

## 1. Purpose and boundary

This contract makes explicit that trusted-time evidence freshness MUST be recomputed at the actual mutation decision boundary, not inherited from a preflight calculation. It extends the P1.8.107/P1.8.112 cross-domain binding and P1.8.93 atomic mutation-fencing requirements.

No clock, TSA, trust anchor, revocation provider, freshness threshold, uncertainty bound, authorization lifetime or adapter is selected. No operation or deletion is authorized.

## 2. Time values are not interchangeable

The decision record MUST retain distinct, typed values for:
- source event time and its stated semantics;
- signed_at and signer identity/key revision;
- TSA genTime and timestamp-token identity;
- received/observed time and the trusted observation source/profile;
- revocation effective time and revocation evidence revision;
- durable intent/commit time and authoritative ordering token;
- restore/reconciliation cut time and governance epoch;
- final mutation-boundary observation time and its uncertainty interval.

A timestamp without its semantic type, source, precision, uncertainty and binding to the exact subject/event is not admissible as a substitute for another time.

## 3. Mandatory final-boundary freshness evaluation

For each exact object mutation, the final decision MUST:
1. Resolve the exact evidence identities and immutable revisions pinned by the protected decision vector.
2. Obtain or validate a trusted current-time observation from an approved, versioned source/profile.
3. Bind the observation to the exact mutation intent, object generation, decision-vector digest and final-fence attempt.
4. Recompute evidence age at that boundary from the typed time values, using the applicable versioned freshness policy and uncertainty model.
5. Re-evaluate certificate/token validity, historical trust status, revocation state, authorization validity and restore/governance epoch constraints.
6. Prove that the trusted observation and all relevant evidence revisions remain valid at the same mutation linearization point, or through a transactionally coupled/cryptographically verifiable ordering protocol.
7. Persist the final observation identity, computed age/uncertainty result, policy revision, decision and ordering token in durable audit evidence before treating the fence as successful.

The age calculation MUST account for observation uncertainty. If the possible age interval intersects an expiry/freshness boundary, or the trusted observation cannot be tied to the mutation decision, the result is UNKNOWN/HOLD. The implementation MUST NOT choose the favorable edge of an uncertainty interval.

## 4. Preflight is not final authorization

A preflight freshness PASS is informational and may be used to avoid unnecessary work. It does not authorize mutation and MUST NOT be reused as the final freshness result.

A final recheck that only confirms the old evidence revision is unchanged is insufficient: time has advanced even if the evidence bytes have not changed.

If obtaining the final trusted-time observation itself requires a network/provider interaction, the design must still prove that the resulting observation is sufficiently bound to the atomic mutation decision. A response received before a later, uncoordinated storage write does not by itself prove freshness at the write's linearization point.

## 5. Fail-closed decision table

| Condition at final fence | Required result |
|---|---|
| Trusted observation, uncertainty, policy and all evidence revisions are valid and atomically bound | May continue to other independent mutation preconditions |
| Trusted time source unavailable or untrusted | HOLD; adapter INELIGIBLE |
| Evidence age cannot be recomputed | HOLD |
| Uncertainty overlaps freshness/validity boundary | UNKNOWN/HOLD |
| Revocation status is stale, missing or conflicting | HOLD |
| Policy/profile revision changed after preflight | Invalidate vector and re-evaluate under new governed decision |
| Observation is valid but not bound to exact intent/object/fence | NOT VERIFIED / HOLD |
| Final check and backend mutation are not atomic or provably ordered | Adapter INELIGIBLE |
| Only preflight freshness PASS exists | Not sufficient; no mutation authorization |

No grace period, freshness duration, uncertainty tolerance or clock-skew value is introduced here.

## 6. Required evidence fields

The final mutation record MUST bind: observation ID; source/profile/version; observed time and precision; uncertainty interval; typed subject time(s); computed age interval; freshness-policy revision; trust/revocation evidence revisions; object identity/generation; mutation intent ID; protected decision-vector digest; governance epoch; final-fence ordering token; result and reason.

## 7. Acceptance criteria

- [x] Preflight and final-boundary freshness are explicitly distinct.
- [x] Age/uncertainty is recomputed at the final mutation decision.
- [x] Observation is bound to exact object, intent and decision vector.
- [x] Atomic/transactional coupling or provable ordering is mandatory.
- [x] Uncertainty overlap, stale trust and unavailable evidence fail closed.
- [x] No numeric parameter/provider is invented.
