## 1. Purpose and precedence

This supplement narrowly closes IR147-01 by making reviewer independence mandatory across all materially conflicting roles and defining an independent second-review control when organizational separation is infeasible.

Apply this supplement with P1.8.146 §6 and the P1.8.144 evidence contract. It does not amend P1.8.143 owner-ratified RET-01 B / RET-02 B, weaken Option C — No Destructive Authority, or supersede stronger separation requirements in upstream contracts. If requirements conflict, the stricter protective requirement applies.

This is a future-assessment documentary control. It does not authorize identity discovery, personnel inspection, permission/API inventory, operational assessment, implementation, tests, workflow dispatch or any data mutation.

## 2. Mandatory independence rule

For each assessment claim and its supporting evidence, the primary independent reviewer MUST be independent of all of the following roles for the assessed matter:

- requester or sponsor of the assessment;
- custodian or controller of the assessed data, copy, source or evidence;
- obligation owner or person responsible for declaring an obligation satisfied/released;
- approver or decision-maker for the assessment outcome;
- executor or operator of any proposed future disposition;
- provider/vendor selector or party responsible for selecting the control/provider whose evidence is being evaluated.

A reviewer holding any listed role for the assessed matter is **CONFLICTED** for that claim. Recording the conflict does not cure it. A conflicted person MUST NOT be the sole reviewer, verifier or accepter of the affected claim.

Independence is assessed against actual decision rights, control, incentives and participation—not title alone. A nominally different account, team or reporting line does not establish independence if the person retains effective control over the relevant evidence or decision.

## 3. Mandatory independent second-review control

Where full separation is infeasible, the assessment MUST NOT waive independence. It MUST use a separately identified independent second reviewer/control that:

1. has none of the conflicting roles listed in §2 for the affected claim;
2. did not create, alter, select, curate or exclusively control the evidence being accepted;
3. has the competence required for the specific claim, or explicitly limits the claim to the competence actually held;
4. independently examines the underlying evidence, scope, provenance, completeness, conflict declarations and proposed disposition—not merely the primary reviewer's conclusion;
5. records its own findings, exceptions, rationale and explicit disposition against the exact evidence version and claim;
6. can reject the claim and preserve BLOCKED / UNKNOWN status without approval from the conflicted role-holder.

The second review is mandatory for every affected claim, not a discretionary compensating control. It must be completed before that claim can be marked independently reviewed. If no qualifying independent reviewer/control is available, the claim remains **INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_REQUIRED / BLOCKED**.

A second reviewer cannot cure missing source evidence, incomplete inventory, unresolved obligations, inadequate legal authority, or a technical control that is NOT VERIFIED. Independence is not a substitute for evidence.

## 4. Role overlap and evidence provenance

The assessment record MUST include a claim-to-role matrix identifying, for each reviewer and evidence producer/controller, whether they performed or controlled any §2 role. Record the role, scope, time period, relevant decision/control, conflict determination and resolution.

For every affected claim, record:
- primary reviewer and independent second reviewer/control, where required;
- exact claim and object/version/copy/graph scope;
- evidence identifiers, immutable/version references and provenance;
- competence basis and any specialist/legal attribution;
- conflict declarations and role-overlap matrix;
- independent second-review procedures and evidence of direct examination;
- findings, dissent, unresolved limitations and final disposition;
- review timestamps and applicable evidence/policy versions.

The person or function responsible for producing evidence may explain its origin but cannot independently validate that evidence as the sole verifier. A custodian or obligation owner may attest to facts within their authority, but their attestation remains evidence to be independently assessed; it is not independent review.

## 5. Competence and specialist boundaries

Independence and competence are separate mandatory tests. Each reviewer must demonstrate competence for the claims they accept. Where a claim requires legal, regulatory, contractual, security-authority, backup/recovery or other specialist judgment, the assessment must attribute the substantive determination to a competent authority or specialist for the applicable domain and jurisdiction.

A technical reviewer may verify the existence, provenance, scope and consistency of specialist evidence but must not manufacture or substitute the substantive determination. A second reviewer who lacks the required competence must narrow the review scope or leave the claim BLOCKED.

Record only role and qualification evidence necessary to establish competence and independence. Do not collect unnecessary personal data, credentials, private contact details or unrelated employment information.

## 6. Fail-closed decision rules

The affected claim MUST remain **INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_REQUIRED / BLOCKED** if any of the following applies:

- reviewer has a conflicting role and no qualifying independent second review is completed;
- second reviewer has any conflicting role or controls the evidence under review;
- role overlap, decision rights or evidence control cannot be established;
- reviewer competence is insufficient or disputed;
- independent examination of underlying evidence cannot be demonstrated;
- primary and second-review findings materially conflict without resolution;
- required specialist/legal attribution is absent;
- evidence version, scope or provenance cannot be bound to the review.

No elapsed time, approval by a conflicted role-holder, organizational seniority, automated check, or agreement between conflicted reviewers converts a blocked claim into an accepted claim.

## 7. No authority expansion

A completed independent review can establish only that the specified documentary claim was independently examined within its stated scope. It does not establish operational inventory completeness, runtime enforcement, deletion eligibility, deletion approval, execution authorization or successful deletion.

P1.8.143 RET-01 B / RET-02 B remain unchanged. T0 canonical data and its availability/recovery copies remain protected under RET-01 B absent a separate new explicit owner decision. CORE_LEDGER and AI_LEDGER remain separate and protected. Option C remains in force; archive and irreversible transitions remain non-executable.

No TTL, retention floor, freshness window, grace period, review cadence, authorization lifetime or trusted-time parameter is selected.
