## 1. Purpose

Specify deterministic semantic fixtures for the highest-risk V07 cases identified in P1.8.36/P1.8.38/P1.8.51/P1.8.56:
- exact signature/context binding;
- timestamp uncertainty against revocation boundaries;
- historical receipt after revocation;
- stale/unknown/unavailable revocation evidence;
- nonce replay and cross-version interoperability.

This specification deliberately separates logical fixture values from cryptographic serialization. Exact TSA profile, imprint algorithm/encoding, signature algorithm, certificate chain and provider remain OPEN. Therefore the fixture cases below are parameterized test specifications, not frozen cryptographic fixture bytes.

## 3. Parameterized time-boundary model

Represent timestamp uncertainty as an interval around the TSA asserted time:

- (T_{low} = genTime - accuracy)
- (T_{high} = genTime + accuracy)

Represent revocation effective time as (T_{rev}) with its declared source precision/uncertainty. Do not treat absent TSA accuracy as zero; use the frozen policy's external accuracy source or mark the interval unresolved.

The fixture oracle must distinguish:
- **Clearly before:** (T_{high} < T_{rev,low})
- **Clearly after:** (T_{low} > T_{rev,high})
- **Overlap/indeterminate:** intervals intersect
- **Exact equality:** depends on the approved precision/equality rule and cannot be inferred from rounded display strings

Until V07 freezes authority, uncertainty, precision and equality semantics, even the “clearly before” cryptographic fixture may only pass binding/token/signature stages. Historical eligibility remains BLOCKED/NOT DETERMINED and final disposition QUARANTINE/HOLD.

## 5. Freeze prerequisites

A case cannot be marked FROZEN until all relevant fields are decided and independently reviewed:
1. exact envelope serialization, domain separation and signature byte extraction;
2. approved digest/signature algorithms and parameters;
3. RFC 3161 profile, TSA policy OID and trust-anchor set;
4. TSA accuracy/uncertainty source and missing-accuracy behavior;
5. authoritative revocation source and signed evidence profile;
6. OCSP nonce profile including RFC 9654 support and responder behavior;
7. time precision, revocation effective-time semantics and equality policy;
8. deterministic test-only key/certificate/token fixture bytes and hashes;
9. stage-separated expected oracle and no-side-effect assertions.

No prerequisite above is implicitly approved by this specification. Provider, trust-anchor, key or external endpoint use requires a separate bounded E2 proposal and explicit owner approval.

## 6. Evidence record format

For each eventual execution, retain:
- fixture ID/version and manifest digest;
- exact source/test SHA and verifier/dependency versions;
- immutable input and expected-output digests;
- token, certificate, OCSP/CRL and response digests;
- recomputed imprint and byte-for-byte comparison result;
- UTC time values, uncertainty interval, source and precision;
- all five stage results and deterministic reason codes;
- stdout/stderr or structured machine-readable results;
- proof that forbidden side effects did not occur;
- evidence class and independent review record.
