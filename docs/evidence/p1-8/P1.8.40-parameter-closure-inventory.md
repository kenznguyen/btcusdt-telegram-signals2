## 1. Purpose and decision boundary

This inventory converts the outstanding V07, V08 and Restore items into individually traceable closure requirements. It identifies the evidence needed, the decision authority and dependencies for each item.

It does not:
- choose a TSA, revocation authority, database, storage provider or deployment topology;
- assign numeric thresholds, precision, uncertainty, cache, propagation, lateness, skew or RPO/RTO values;
- freeze executable fixture bytes or expected outcomes;
- establish environment equivalence or production assurance;
- authorize read probes, key operations, provisioning, staging, restore, fault injection or workflow dispatch.

The approved policy directions remain binding:
- V07 / DEC-01: independently verifiable trusted time; fail-closed and quarantine when trust/revocation cannot be proven.
- V08 / DEC-02: UTC half-open windows, monotonic source-progress watermark, durable commit-time gate, immutable sealed checkpoints and append-only correction path.
- Restore / DEC-03: explicit failure-domain and measured ACK/RPO/RTO contract; existing targets remain planning targets only.

## 2. Status vocabulary

- **OPEN:** no exact value/contract has been approved or evidenced.
- **BLOCKED:** dependent work cannot pass until the item is resolved.
- **EVIDENCE REQUIRED:** a measurement or implementation/provider contract must be supplied before a value can be proposed.
- **OWNER DECISION REQUIRED:** an explicit owner decision is needed after evidence and alternatives are presented.
- **READY FOR REVIEW:** all documentation and evidence inputs are assembled for independent review; this is not execution authorization.

No silence, “continue”, or completion of this inventory is treated as approval of a parameter.
