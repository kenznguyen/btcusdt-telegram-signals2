## 5. Mandatory safety invariants

1. A timestamp proves only the scoped existence claim supported by a valid token at its asserted `genTime`; it does not prove exact client-side signature creation time or signer intent.
2. Timestamp imprint must bind exact signature bytes and required immutable context.
3. Client-supplied `signed_at` and local receipt time cannot substitute for trusted signing-time proof.
4. Binding/token/signature cryptographic PASS cannot automatically promote historical eligibility or final ACCEPT.
5. Unknown, stale, unavailable, contradictory or unverifiable trust/revocation state remains fail-closed and quarantined.
6. Timestamp uncertainty overlapping a key-validity/revocation boundary remains HOLD/quarantine until exact semantics are approved.
7. No private key, credential or secret material may enter fixtures, logs or evidence artifacts.
