## 2. Findings and closure

### IR149-01 — CLOSED at documentary specification level

P1.8.150 resolves the exact ambiguity identified in P1.8.149:

1. **Capacity separation:** §2 distinguishes a CONFLICTED CONTRIBUTOR / evidence producer from a QUALIFYING INDEPENDENT REVIEWER.
2. **No review authority for conflicted contributor:** the conflicted participant may provide evidence inputs or preliminary analysis only when labelled NON-INDEPENDENT — NON-AUTHORITATIVE — NOT AN ACCEPTANCE. They cannot issue, approve, co-sign, veto or override the independent disposition.
3. **Second-review semantics:** §3 expressly defines “independent second reviewer” as procedural sequencing terminology in this scenario, not as recognition of the conflicted participant as a qualifying first reviewer.
4. **Sole qualifying disposition:** the independent second reviewer becomes the sole qualifying independent reviewer for the affected claim and directly examines underlying evidence, exact scope/version, provenance, completeness, role declarations and limitations.
5. **Fail-closed:** if no qualifying independent reviewer is available, the claim remains INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_REQUIRED / BLOCKED. A second conflicted person, mutual approval, seniority, elapsed time or automated check cannot substitute.
6. **Evidence traceability:** §4 requires participant capacities, role/conflict matrix, exact scope, evidence provenance, direct-examination proof, competence basis and final disposition to be recorded.
7. **No proxy acceptance:** §5 prevents a conflicted contribution, signature or recommendation from being counted as independent acceptance.

The new rules are consistent with P1.8.148 §§2–6. P1.8.150 does not waive the mandatory independent second review or permit conflicted participants to validate one another. Its direct-evidence requirement is at least as protective as the upstream contract.
