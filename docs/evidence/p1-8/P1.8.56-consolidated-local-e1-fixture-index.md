## 2. Assurance and identity rules

- **E1/SYNTHETIC** means deterministic local/reference-model evidence only.
- Every fixture requires a stable ID, version, immutable input digest, source/test SHA, deterministic setup, expected oracle, observed result, and evidence reference.
- Keep base-event identity separate from delivery/retry identity.
- Negative fixtures must prove both rejection/quarantine behavior and absence of forbidden side effects.
- A fixture without frozen bytes and a machine-checkable oracle is SPECIFIED, not FROZEN.
- A fixture without a recorded execution bound to exact source SHA is NOT EXECUTED.
- E1 PASS cannot be promoted to E2, E3, E4 or production assurance.
