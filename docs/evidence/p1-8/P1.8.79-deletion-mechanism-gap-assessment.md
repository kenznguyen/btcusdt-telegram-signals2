## 4. Confirmed design strengths

The following controls are sufficiently represented at design level and should not be weakened in subsequent work:

1. Fail-closed on missing, stale, contradictory, unavailable, or unverifiable evidence.
2. No automatic deletion from age, TTL, low storage, no reads, ACK, timeout, job completion, or archive completion.
3. Eligibility is not approval; approval is not execution authorization.
4. Exact immutable dataset/version/object scope; no wildcard or mutable alias.
5. Requester and approver must be separate; executor cannot make policy decisions.
6. Consumer ACK is not dependency release or deletion permission.
7. Missing heartbeat, stale consumer, or unknown owner never auto-releases dependency.
8. No implicit cascade from source to backup, replica, export, cache, evidence, derived dataset, model, or ledger.
9. Ambiguous execution outcome requires reconciliation before retry.
10. Destructive completion requires independent post-operation verification.
11. T0 writer fencing is independent of deletion governance and cannot substitute for approval.
12. `AI_EXECUTION_ENABLED=false`; no live trading boundary is changed.

## 5. Blocking decisions and evidence

The following remain blockers; this assessment does not select values or infer approval:

- Which dataset classes may ever be deleted, archived, or permanently protected.
- Whether T0 canonical market data is absolutely non-deletable or has a narrow exception path.
- Normative consumer availability requirements for archive compatibility.
- Authoritative registry and audit-store consistency/availability guarantees.
- Approver quorum, delegated authority, and safe separation-of-duties model for a personal system.
- Grace period, cancellation deadline, authorization lifetime, evidence freshness window.
- Tombstone/audit retention and privacy policy.
- Emergency/legal request governance.
- Exact manifest encoding, canonicalization, digest, and object-resolution contract.
- Adapter-specific safeguards and independent verification oracles.
- Runtime inventory and restore/rebuild evidence by dataset and copy class.
- Deterministic acceptance vectors and separately authorized non-production validation.
