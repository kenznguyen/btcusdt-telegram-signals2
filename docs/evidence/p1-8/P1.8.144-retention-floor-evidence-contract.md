# P1.8.144 — Retention Floor Evidence Contract

Date: 2026-11-09  
Status: **DOCUMENTATION DRAFT COMPLETE — INDEPENDENT REVIEW REQUIRED — NOT EXECUTED**

## 1. Purpose

Define the evidence contract for establishing a minimum retention floor for an exact data object/version or copy. The contract prevents arbitrary TTL selection and prevents consumer completion, age, storage pressure or technical convenience from being treated as proof that retention obligations have ended.

This document is a design contract only. It does not set numeric retention periods, approve a deletion class/object, establish legal advice, authorize inventory or data reads, qualify a storage adapter, or grant archive/deletion authority.

## 2. Governing decisions and compatibility

This contract is subordinate to:

- P1.8.142 — Owner Retention and Data-Class Disposition Decision Package.
- P1.8.143 — Owner Retention Policy Ratification.

Ratified policy constraints:

- RET-01 B: T0 canonical data is protected by default; no routine or automatic deletion. A narrowly scoped binding legal/security exception can only be considered through separate evidence, competent review and a new explicit owner decision.
- RET-02 B: only the limited noncanonical classes identified in P1.8.143 may enter future exact-object/version read-only eligibility assessment.
- No numeric TTL, retention floor, grace period, review cadence, authorization lifetime or trusted-time freshness value has been approved.
- Archive remains non-executable. Destructive authority remains NOT GRANTED.

If this contract conflicts with a higher-level approved policy, the stricter protective interpretation applies and the conflict must be escalated; this document cannot amend owner policy.

## 3. Terms and distinctions

- **Retention obligation:** a reason an object/version/copy must remain available or recoverable.
- **Retention floor:** the earliest point at which all applicable minimum retention obligations are demonstrably satisfied. It is not a deletion date or authorization.
- **Obligation end condition:** an attributable, evidence-backed event or decision that closes one specific obligation.
- **Review eligibility:** permission to conduct a read-only assessment for an exact object/version. It is not deletion eligibility or execution permission.
- **Evidence completeness:** proof that the inventory of applicable obligations, dependencies, copies and holds is sufficiently complete to support the stated claim.
- **Copy lineage:** a traceable relationship from a copy to its source/version, transformations, custody and downstream copies.
- **Unknown:** any obligation, dependency, hold, lineage, custodian, copy or end condition that is absent, ambiguous, stale or unverified.

A retention floor can be calculated only when the obligation set is complete enough for the claim. Unknown is protective, not zero.

## 4. Core determination rule

For an exact object/version/copy, define the applicable obligation set as O = {o1 ... on}. Each obligation must have:

1. a stable obligation ID and category;
2. an exact object/version/copy scope;
3. an accountable owner/custodian and authoritative source;
4. a start/basis and applicable policy/version;
5. a documented end condition;
6. evidence proving that end condition;
7. evidence provenance, integrity and verification status;
8. any dependent objects, copies or holds that remain protective.

The candidate retention floor is the latest proven end among all applicable minimum-retention obligations:

`candidate_floor = max(proven_end(o_i))`

This expression is valid only if:

- the obligation inventory is complete for the declared scope;
- every applicable obligation has a valid, attributable end condition;
- no active or unresolved hold, dependency, recovery requirement or legal/security restriction remains;
- trusted-time and ordering evidence meet the applicable approved contract;
- copy/lineage closure is established for the claim being made.

If any obligation has no approved end condition, any end is unproven, the inventory is incomplete, or a clock/order/lineage fact is uncertain, the result is **FLOOR_NOT_ESTABLISHED / RETAIN / BLOCKED**. Do not substitute a default duration or infer a floor from object age.

A floor only states that known minimum obligations have been met. It does not prove that retention is no longer useful, that deletion is lawful, that the object is eligible for deletion, or that mutation is authorized.

## 5. Required obligation domains and evidence

The assessment must consider each domain below and explicitly mark it APPLICABLE, NOT APPLICABLE with rationale, or UNKNOWN. Omission is not equivalent to NOT APPLICABLE.

| Domain | Evidence needed to establish an end condition | Fail-closed condition |
|---|---|---|
| Legal, regulatory, contractual and privacy | Applicable jurisdiction/contract/policy, competent authority, effective version, hold/release instrument and scope | Authority, jurisdiction, applicability or release unclear |
| Canonical/source obligation | Data-class contract, canonical owner, source-of-truth designation and approved lifecycle rule | Canonical status or owner unclear; T0 protected by RET-01 |
| Consumer dependencies | Exact consumer/object/version references, acknowledged release records, independent dependency-registry state | Consumer unknown/stale, registry unavailable, release ambiguous |
| Replay and correction | Replay horizon/baseline, correction and late-event obligations, reproducibility proof | Replay/correction path still needed or unmeasured |
| Training, inference and evaluation | Dataset/model/run lineage, training/evaluation use, model decision dependencies and reproducibility evidence | Model or result provenance incomplete; output supports material decision/claim |
| Backtest and research | Exact experiment/run lineage, reproducibility inputs and retained evidence needed to reproduce conclusions | Research result cannot be reconstructed or is still relied upon |
| Trading and paper-outcome reconstruction | Signal, admission, risk, order-intent/paper-event and ledger references needed to reconstruct outcomes | CORE_LEDGER/AI_LEDGER or required T3 evidence involved; reconstruction incomplete |
| Audit, security and incident | Audit/incident scope, investigation status, evidence preservation instruction and authorized release | Open incident, audit request, security hold or missing release |
| Recovery and backup chain | Recoverable chain membership, snapshot/transaction boundary, restore obligation, independent integrity/reconciliation proof | Backup chain, restore point or recovery obligation unresolved |
| Replicas and failover | Replica identity, source/version relation, failover/rebuild role and verified replacement evidence | Replica is needed for availability or replacement not verified |
| Export and external recipient | Export identity, recipient/custodian, onward-copy accounting and authoritative disposition evidence | Recipient/copies unknown or transfer/deletion assertion unverified |
| Governance and lifecycle evidence | Policy/approval/manifest/dependency/hold/tombstone/audit evidence relationships and historical verification obligations | Evidence is needed to prove authority, process or outcome |
| Copy lineage and transformations | Complete source-to-copy graph, transformation versions, copy completeness and custody | Orphan, missing edge, unknown location or unverified completeness |
| Availability and service obligations | Consumer availability contract, latency/availability obligations and replacement/rebuild acceptance | Availability impact unknown or consumer contract open |

No single ACK, last-access timestamp, successful job, model completion, checkpoint, provider expiry, object age or backup age closes these domains by itself.

## 6. Class-specific application

The class matrix below controls which evidence can be considered; it does not confer disposition authority.

### 6.1 T0 canonical market data

- Protected by default under RET-01 B.
- No routine retention-floor calculation may be used to create an automatic deletion path.
- Ordinary cleanup, TTL, archive or deletion eligibility is prohibited.
- A binding legal/security exception requires a separate exact-scope proposal, competent review, independent evidence and a new explicit owner decision. Until then: PROTECTED / NO ROUTINE DISPOSITION.

### 6.2 T1 raw observations

- May enter only a future read-only exact-object/version assessment under RET-02 B.
- Must prove exact downstream reproducibility from retained authoritative inputs, including transformations, time boundaries, late/corrected events and version lineage.
- Must prove all consumers, research, audit, incident, recovery and copy obligations have closed.
- If T1 data is the only evidence supporting an output or reconstruction, retain it.

### 6.3 T2 ephemeral inference outputs

- May enter only a future read-only exact-object/version assessment.
- Must prove the output is not a material input to a signal, risk/admission decision, ledger/paper outcome, model evaluation, audit or claim that must be reconstructed.
- Must preserve model/version/configuration/input lineage and any required decision evidence.
- “Inference completed” or “consumed” is not an end condition.

### 6.4 T3 events, reconciliation and ledgers

- CORE_LEDGER and AI_LEDGER remain separate and protected.
- Required paper-outcome, risk-decision, reconciliation, audit and incident records remain protected while any reconstruction or governance obligation exists.
- This contract does not place ledger records into the RET-02 reviewable set.

### 6.5 T4 caches, read models and materializations

- May enter only a future read-only exact-object/version assessment.
- Must prove deterministic rebuildability from retained authoritative inputs, exact transformation/version, source availability, rebuild correctness and consumer availability.
- Must prove no independent evidentiary value, external export dependency, hold or recovery role.
- A theoretical ability to rebuild is insufficient; proof must be tied to the exact version and declared rebuild claim.

### 6.6 Copies, backups, replicas and exports

- Every copy is assessed individually with its own identity, lineage, custodian, location, completeness, dependencies, holds and recovery role.
- Source and copy do not share an assumed retention floor.
- A source obligation does not automatically close when one copy is removed; a copy does not become disposable merely because the source exists.
- Backup chains are assessed as recoverable units, not isolated file ages.
- No cascade disposition is permitted.

### 6.7 Governance, evidence and unknown data

- Policy, approvals, manifests, dependency/hold records, tombstones, audit records and evidence copies remain protected while needed to prove lifecycle authority and outcomes.
- Unknown, unclassified, orphaned or incompletely inventoried data is BLOCKED_UNKNOWN and cannot be presumed ephemeral.

## 7. Time, ordering and policy-version requirements

A retention determination must identify the time basis used for each end condition: event time, trusted receipt time, durable commit time, legal effective time or another explicitly defined clock.

- Do not compare timestamps from different domains without an approved binding/crosswalk.
- Preserve timezone, precision, uncertainty, source identity and proof provenance.
- If trusted-time uncertainty overlaps a boundary or event ordering cannot be proven, retain/hold.
- A policy change cannot be applied retroactively without explicit transition rules and owner approval.
- A later policy version does not silently shorten an already applicable obligation.
- P1.8.107, P1.8.110, P1.8.112 and P1.8.117 trusted-time/restore/mutation cross-domain contracts remain controlling; unresolved parameters remain OPEN/BLOCKED.
- No numeric freshness, skew, grace or retention values are introduced by this contract.

## 8. Minimum evidence record

Every future assessment record must contain, at minimum:

1. assessment ID and immutable policy/contract version;
2. exact data class, object ID, version/generation and copy ID where applicable;
3. source dataset/version and full known lineage references;
4. accountable data owner, custodian and assessment requester;
5. declared scope and explicit exclusions;
6. obligation matrix covering every domain in §5;
7. evidence references, content digests, provenance and verification results;
8. dependency, consumer, hold, recovery and copy graph closure evidence;
9. trusted-time basis, uncertainty and ordering proof;
10. each obligation's end condition and its independent evidence;
11. candidate floor or explicit NOT_ESTABLISHED result, with rationale;
12. unresolved assumptions, unknowns, conflicts and stale observations;
13. independent reviewer identity/role and review result;
14. explicit statement that the result is read-only and conveys no mutation authority.

Do not copy secret values, credentials or unnecessary sensitive payloads into the record. Evidence references should be minimized while remaining independently verifiable.

## 9. State machine and stop conditions

Permitted documentary/read-only assessment outcomes:

- **PROTECTED** — policy excludes the class/object from ordinary review.
- **BLOCKED_UNKNOWN** — inventory, lineage, owner or obligation coverage incomplete.
- **RETAIN** — at least one active or unresolved obligation/hold/dependency remains.
- **FLOOR_NOT_ESTABLISHED** — no defensible floor can be calculated from available evidence.
- **FLOOR_ESTABLISHED_FOR_REVIEW** — all applicable minimum obligations have proven end conditions for the declared scope; this is not deletion approval.
- **INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_REQUIRED** — evidence package awaits review or has findings.

Any conflict, registry failure, stale/unknown consumer, missing copy, ambiguous timestamp, incomplete recovery chain, missing authority, or unexplained evidence gap results in PROTECTED/RETAIN/BLOCKED, not a best-effort estimate.

A review date creates only a future owner review task. It cannot expire a hold, release a dependency or trigger a lifecycle mutation.
