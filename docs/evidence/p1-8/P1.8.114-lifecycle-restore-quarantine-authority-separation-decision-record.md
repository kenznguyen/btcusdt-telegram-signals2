# P1.8.114 — Lifecycle / Restore / Quarantine Authority Separation Decision Record

**Version:** P1.8.114-v1  
**Closes:** P1.8.109 IR109-05 (MAJOR)  
**Depends on:** Data Deletion Safety Contract, Central Data Lifecycle Policy, Dataset Consumer Contract, P1.8.93, P1.8.103, P1.8.105, P1.8.107–P1.8.109  
**Type:** Documentation-only authority decision record  
**Status:** ROLE BOUNDARIES RECORDED — OWNER ROLE-MODEL CHOICE OPEN — NO DELETION AUTHORITY

## 1. Purpose and decision boundary

This record separates recovery readiness, V07 quarantine resolution, TSA evidence verification, lifecycle eligibility, deletion approval, execution authorization and storage execution.

It records mandatory non-escalation boundaries. It does **not** claim that the owner has selected named people, teams, credentials, a dual-control model or a single-owner compensating model. That choice remains OPEN and requires a separate explicit owner decision supported by conflict-of-interest and assurance evidence.

No role, permission, credential, provider or operational workflow is provisioned by this document.

## 2. Binding non-escalation decisions

The following boundaries are mandatory:

1. Restore READY means only that the exact restored scope passed its recovery-readiness criteria. It is not lifecycle eligibility, deletion approval or execution authorization.
2. V07 quarantine resolution means only that the exact V07 evidence/key-status question was resolved under its approved policy. It does not make an object lifecycle-eligible.
3. TSA historical verification means only that a bounded historical evidence claim was reproduced or assigned an explicit indeterminate result. It does not release holds or authorize disposition.
4. V08 checkpoint closure is only one lifecycle evidence input. It does not release consumers or remove replay, training, backtest, audit or recovery demand.
5. Lifecycle eligibility is a central policy decision for an exact dataset/version/object/copy scope. It is not approval to perform a destructive operation.
6. Approval is a distinct authorization decision over an immutable package and exact operation/scope.
7. Execution authorization is a separately issued, revocable, short-scope capability. It is not the storage mutation itself.
8. Storage execution is limited to an independently qualified adapter and must pass P1.8.93 atomic mutation fencing.
9. Independent post-operation verification is required before any completion claim.
10. No stage may infer authority from another stage's status, role, service identity or successful technical result.

## 3. Required authority domains

| Authority domain | Permitted decision (future, subject to owner-approved role model) | Explicitly not permitted |
|---|---|---|
| Restore readiness authority | READY/NOT_READY for exact restored scope after P1.8.105 reconciliation | Lifecycle eligibility, approval, execution authorization, storage mutation |
| V07 quarantine-resolution authority | Resolve or retain exact V07 quarantine under approved trust/revocation policy | Restore READY, lifecycle approval, key operation outside separately authorized procedure |
| TSA evidence verifier | Verify exact evidence manifest and report PASS/INDETERMINATE/FAIL | Clear holds, release dependencies, approve deletion |
| V08/checkpoint verifier | Report checkpoint closure and unresolved source/commit/correction state | Release consumer dependencies or authorize deletion |
| Consumer dependency owner | Release only its own exact dependency with versioned evidence | Release another consumer's dependency or global lifecycle hold |
| Lifecycle assessor | Determine eligibility/ineligibility for exact scope under central policy | Approve or execute destructive operation |
| Independent approver | Approve/reject immutable package within delegated scope | Alter evidence, widen scope, execute storage mutation |
| Authorization authority | Issue/revoke exact operation/object/generation/executor capability after approval | Perform storage mutation or self-approve outside an explicitly approved exception model |
| Storage executor/adapter | Execute only the exact authorized operation after atomic fence | Decide eligibility, approve, widen scope or clear holds |
| Independent verifier/auditor | Verify outcome/evidence and report discrepancy | Rewrite operation history or grant retroactive authority |

The same principal MUST NOT combine incompatible domains by default. Any proposed exception requires a separately approved compensating-control model; no exception is inferred here.

## 4. Separation and independence requirements

A future role model must document:
- principal identity, authentication strength and lifecycle;
- exact permitted action and scope;
- policy revision and delegation chain;
- independence from the evidence or operation being reviewed;
- conflict-of-interest checks and self-approval prevention;
- revocation and emergency suspension;
- immutable audit of decision, evidence and reason;
- coverage for absence, outage, compromise and role handover;
- independent verification of role assignment and actual effective permissions.

A label such as “admin”, “owner”, “operator”, “service account” or “independent” is not proof of separation. Distinct names or accounts are insufficient if they share effective credentials, control or approval authority.

## 5. Single-owner / compensating-control option remains undecided

P1.8.103 left the independent-approver versus single-owner compensating-control model OPEN. This record does not choose either option.

Before any single-owner exception could be considered, a separate owner decision would need to specify at minimum: why independent approval is unavailable; exact limited scope; mandatory delay or independent evidence review where appropriate; immutable pre-commit and post-commit audit; out-of-band notification; emergency stop/revocation; periodic independent retrospective review; and conditions that suspend the exception. The controls and any values must be evidence-based and explicitly approved.

Absent such a decision, the default is **no self-approval and no combining incompatible authority domains**.

## 6. Required state machine and blocking semantics

| State/result | Meaning | Permitted next step |
|---|---|---|
| RESTORE_NOT_READY / QUARANTINED | Recovery evidence incomplete or ambiguous | Reconcile; no consumers or destructive progression |
| RESTORE_READY | Exact recovery scope passed recovery gate | Normal read/use subject to policy; no deletion authority |
| V07_QUARANTINED | Trust/revocation question unresolved | Preserve quarantine; no dependent destructive decision |
| V07_RESOLVED | Exact V07 question resolved under policy | Re-evaluate lifecycle independently |
| TSA_INDETERMINATE | Historical evidence incomplete/unverifiable | Preserve evidence and dependent claims |
| V08_BLOCKED | Checkpoint/source/commit/correction closure incomplete | Resolve evidence; no lifecycle closure claim |
| LIFECYCLE_INELIGIBLE | Demand, hold, dependency or policy blocker remains | Preserve object and dependencies |
| LIFECYCLE_ELIGIBLE | Central assessor reports exact scope eligible | May submit separate approval package; no execution |
| APPROVED | Independent approval of exact immutable package | May request separate execution authorization |
| AUTHORIZED | Exact operation/object/generation/executor authorization issued | Adapter may attempt final atomic fence |
| EXECUTION_COMMITTED / UNKNOWN | Backend outcome recorded or ambiguous | Independent verification/reconciliation |
| VERIFIED | Exact postcondition independently proven | Close only that operation record; no cascade |

A transition is blocked if its prerequisite evidence or authority is missing, stale, conflicting or outside exact scope. No timeout, role availability, successful restore, verifier PASS or workflow status advances a state automatically.

## 7. Conflict and emergency handling

- Conflicting authority decisions produce HOLD and independent review; no last-writer-wins rule.
- Revocation or suspension of a principal blocks future actions at the authoritative fence.
- Emergency access may stop or contain activity but cannot silently create deletion eligibility or bypass audit.
- If an operation may already have committed, loss of authority or evidence continuity requires reconciliation, not a blind retry or inferred rollback.
- Any compensating control must be explicitly versioned, scoped, time-bounded only if an owner-approved value exists, and independently reviewable.
