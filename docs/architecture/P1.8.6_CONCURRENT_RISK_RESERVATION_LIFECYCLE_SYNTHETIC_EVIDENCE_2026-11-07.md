Implemented contracts:
- Serialize concurrent admissions with a process-local re-entrant lock.
- Concurrent distinct intents cannot exceed total exposure or open-reservation limits.
- Concurrent duplicate delivery of one identical intent creates one reservation and returns the same decision.
- Reservation release is idempotent and returns reserved capacity.
- Expiry uses explicit timezone-aware UTC timestamps and releases reservations at or before the deadline.
- Snapshot export/restore preserves active/released reservation states and intent idempotency records.
- Snapshot SHA-256 integrity and basic reservation-ledger/risk-limit consistency are checked during restore.
- Replay of an already accepted intent after release/restart returns its original decision without reactivating the reservation.
