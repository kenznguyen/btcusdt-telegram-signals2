## Approval and scope

User approval: `phê duyệt` for P1.8.8 proposed scope.

Implemented a bounded recovery acceptance matrix for the existing synthetic file-backed Shared Risk reference model:
- Fault injection after temporary-file write, after temporary-file `fsync`, before atomic replacement, after atomic replacement, and immediately before parent-directory `fsync`.
- Repeated process restart/reopen/recovery cycles with reservation ledger and exposure reconciliation.
- Recovery validates the authoritative committed journal before touching temporary files.
- Recovery removes only orphan files matching `.risk-state-*.tmp`, then fsyncs the containing directory.
- Missing or corrupt committed state is rejected and left untouched; recovery does not silently initialize a fresh journal.
- Recovery checks active reservation count and reserved exposure across restart cycles.

The five fault boundaries model process termination only. They do not simulate power loss, kernel panic, storage controller cache loss, or filesystem corruption.
